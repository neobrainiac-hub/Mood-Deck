MOOD DECK — DAY 15: YOUR NEXT GENTLE STEP

Based on the Day 14 Personal Experience project.

WHAT'S NEW
- After a mood check-in, a new card appears on the home page: "You've checked in. What do you need now?"
- Offers three optional next steps based on the mood category:
  * Reflect (Journal)
  * Find a word (The Word / Bible)
  * Pause (Ripple Room)
  * Give thanks (Gratitude Garden)
  * Play (Mindful Memory)
- Suggestions are optional and use gentle, non-prescriptive language.
- Card appears only when the newest check-in is from today.

FILES
- index.html
- scripts/prototype.js
- styles/main.css
All other files and existing assets from Day 14 are preserved.

INSTALL
1. Extract into a new folder.
2. Open the folder in VS Code.
3. Use Live Server to open index.html.
4. Make a mood check-in and save it.
5. On the home page, test the three next-step buttons.
6. Test reflect (Journal), Scripture (The Word), Pause (Ripple Room), gratitude (Garden), and Play (Mindful Memory) recommendations as shown for your mood category.
NOTE
This is a local prototype; the feature is not clinical advice and does not diagnose or treat anything.
