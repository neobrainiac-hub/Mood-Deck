MOOD DECK · DAY 17
A more personal weekly reflection

This update builds on the Day 16 Understanding Your Patterns project.

What changed in Insights:
- Weekly mood mix, activity chart and recurring influences now use the last seven calendar days.
- The reflection summarises actual recent check-ins, most-recorded mood, average intensity and active days.
- Repeated influences are highlighted only when they occur more than once.
- A comparison with the previous seven days appears only when there are at least three check-ins in each period.
- Empty and low-data states explain when there is not enough information yet.
- Historical entries remain available in All check-ins; existing localStorage keys and other pages are unchanged.

TEST CHECKLIST
1. Open Insights with no check-ins in the last seven days; confirm the empty state.
2. Add one or two check-ins; confirm the low-data message.
3. Add at least three check-ins with different moods, intensities and influences.
4. Confirm the chart, emotion mix, reflection and influences reflect only the last seven days.
5. Add at least three entries in the previous seven-day period (or use existing dated entries) to test the comparison.
6. Refresh and confirm data remains saved.
7. Check Bible, Sound, Journal, Elo, Mood Play and navigation still open as before.

Note: The reflection is a deterministic summary of user-entered data, not AI, diagnosis or an explanation of emotional causes.
